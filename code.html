The Architecture of Taste

Engineered for pure tactile & aromatic indulgence.

Every slice of L'Opéra Noir is structured according to precise pastry physics: balancing the bitter density of dark cocoa crumb with the light, melting sensation of whipped ganache mousse and the delicate snap of tempered chocolate.

Layer 01 • The Crown diamond

Miroir Noir Glaze & Tablet

A high-gloss mirror glaze poured at precisely 32°C, topped with a rosette of whipped cocoa buttercream and a snap-tempered dark chocolate tablet.

Notes: Crisp initial snap, bittersweet gloss, cacao nib crunch
Layer 02 • The Body grain

Whipped Guanaja Ganache

Two central strata of aerated 70% dark chocolate mousse emulsified with cultured Normandy cream, delivering a silky melt-in-your-mouth sensation.

Notes: Creamy density, floral vanilla warmth, rounded acidity
Layer 03 • The Foundation water_drop

Dark Cocoa Génoise

Three tiers of slow-baked sponge enriched with Dutch alkalized cocoa powder and brushed with Madagascar planifolia bourbon vanilla syrup.

Notes: Moist airy crumb, deep roasted cacao tones, lingering finish
